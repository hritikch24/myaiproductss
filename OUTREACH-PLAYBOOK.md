## Daily Action Plan (1 hour/day)

### Morning (30 min) — Find & DM
1. Pick ONE niche for the day (rotate through the list)
2. Search 3 hashtags from that niche
3. Open 10 profiles that match:
   - Active (posted in last 7 days)
   - 1K-50K followers
   - No website link in bio (or Linktree only)
   - Location: any Indian city
4. Send the Cold DM (below) to all 10

### Evening (30 min) — Follow up & engage
1. Reply to any responses from earlier DMs
2. Check yesterday's DMs — send Follow-up 1 if no reply
3. Like + comment on 5 recent posts of people you DMed (genuine comments, not "nice pic")

## Objection Handling

**"₹9,999 is too expensive"**
> Totally understand. But think about it — if your website gets you even ONE extra client per month, that's ₹15,000-50,000 in revenue. The site pays for itself with the first booking. And there are no monthly fees unless you want maintenance.

**"I'll think about it"**
> Of course! No pressure. Tell you what — let me build the free mockup anyway. You can see exactly what your site would look like, and then decide. Zero risk for you.

**"I already have Instagram"**
> Instagram is amazing for showcasing work! But here's the thing — 76% of clients Google you before booking. If they find nothing, they go to someone who does have a site. Your website works while you sleep, while Instagram only works when you post.

**"My friend's brother can do it cheaper"**
> He probably can! But will he do Google Business setup? Will he optimize for "makeup artist in Jaipur" so you rank on Google? Will he still be available 3 months later if you need a change? We include all of that, and you see the mockup free before paying.

**"I don't have time for this"**
> That's the best part — it takes 5 minutes from you. Send me your best photos and your service list on WhatsApp. We do everything else. You don't need to write content, choose colors, or learn any tech.
